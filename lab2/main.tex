\section*{Behavior and Implementation}
In this lab, we were able to program the nao to detect when a person is face to face with the Nao. When this condition is met, the Nao will greet the person in front of it and ask for their name. The nao will then try to listen to the person's name and when they leave they will say "Goodbye {person's name}". When implementing this program we noticed that the Nao would occasionally recenter it's facial orientation to focus on the person's face. Sometimes the Nao had difficulty recognizing what people were saying to the Nao, sometimes it would hear what the person in front was saying as well as the people around it and other times it wouldn't recognize anything anyone said even though the computer microphone was close to the user.  \\

Implementation wise, we modified code to go from simply detecting a person in front of the Nao, to first using text to speech to say "Hi, What is your name?" and then listening for the response. Afterwards it would store whatever the person said as well as the person ID. When the person would leave, the code would recall the ID and say "Goodbye" with whatever the person said.
